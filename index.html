THE NEED FOR A GREENER CAMPUS

Why does IIT Roorkee need
a Green Corridor?

Green corridors can connect vegetated spaces and provide shade along selected roads and pedestrian routes. Our project uses GIS and remote sensing to identify locations where green infrastructure may be most beneficial.
